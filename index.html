About Smart Cradle AI

Building Smarter Baby Care with AI & IoT — SIH Hackathon Prototype

The Problem

Parents and caregivers struggle to continuously monitor infants, leading to fatigue and delayed intervention. Standard cradles move blindly without understanding why a baby is crying—whether due to hunger, sleepiness, discomfort, or health distress.

The AI Solution

Smart Cradle AI integrates acoustic cry classification with environmental IoT telemetry. By recognizing emotional and physiological need patterns, the cradle automatically adjusts soothing motions, plays soothing audio, and alerts parents immediately.

Key Innovations

  • Emotion-Aware Classification: Deep learning model trained on acoustic cry spectral features.
  • Closed-Loop Automation: Dynamic PWM motor speed scaling matching detected distress level.
  • Multi-Sensor Telemetry: DHT22 ambient comfort check + MPU6050 physical movement tracking.

Future Roadmap

  • Edge Impulse TinyML deployment directly on ESP32-S3 chip.
  • Mobile app with push notifications & GSM emergency fallback.
  • Personalized infant learning model adapting to individual baby cry profiles.
